# Homepage visual assets

- **knowledge-constellation.webp**: original concept artwork generated with the built-in image-generation tool on October 30, 2026. The image is illustrative, not a photograph of a product or a historical artifact. The delivered PNG was encoded as WebP for the website; no semantic edits were made.
- **harness-workshop.jpg**: screenshot of the actual public Harness workshop, captured while preparing an example. No AI conversation was started and no private documents were uploaded.
- The two homepage typefaces reuse the repository's Cormorant Garamond and IBM Plex Mono files, with their existing license documents in [book/assets/fonts](../../book/assets/fonts/).

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium modern AI platform homepage visual for COMPANION, an open-source protocol for structured dialogue with AI-generated historical perspectives. Generate one wide 3:2 image, no lettering anywhere. A refined sculptural study of three distinct human profiles formed from many fine layered bronze and ivory sheets, like an archive giving form to thought. A classical philosopher profile, a nineteenth-century woman mathematician profile, and an abstract contemporary profile, neither literal portraits nor photoreal people. Arrange as an elegant horizontal constellation, connected by a few very fine warm-gold threads to small floating translucent document sheets, with generous negative space. Deep charcoal nearly black studio background, soft directional light, warm pale bronze highlights, understated amber edge light. Material precision of a high-end physical computing installation, sharp fine craftsmanship, quiet and intellectually inviting. Restrained warm palette; no neon, blue or purple gradients, no occult symbols, no robots, no circuit-board brain, no marketing text, no UI, no logos, no watermark. The object should feel like the encounter of human knowledge and a powerful contemporary tool, not a book cover or fantasy artwork. Main composition centred with safe margins so the image can be used as a homepage feature and a README masthead.
