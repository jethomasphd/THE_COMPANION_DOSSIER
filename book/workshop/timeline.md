# USING THIS MATTER · A Timeline of the Record

An in-narrative chronology, built so a reader can ground themselves at any point in the book **without being told anything the record has not yet told them.**

**How to use this page.** The timeline is layered. Read the first layer before page one if you like; it contains only what the volume's own front matter and jacket already say. Each later layer is gated: open it only after you finish the Record it is named for. Nothing inside a gate spoils anything beyond that gate.

*Revised for the final edition. Every date below has been checked against the manuscript and, where the record touches the real estate, against the repository itself. The novel's real-world record ends on **16 October 2026**, the last printed page of the Watchtower binders. Everything before that date can be checked in the public estate. Everything after it is the fiction.*

---

## The estate's calendar (safe from page one)

The estate does not count in calendar years. It counts in springs, from its own threshold: the founding year is the first spring.

| The estate says | The calendar says |
|---|---|
| the first spring | 2025 · the founding year (the estate is founded in its December) |
| the second spring · the second vigil | 2026 · the war, the false peace, and the nineteen sessions |
| the third spring | 2027 · the season of the appraisal and the deposit |
| the fourth spring | 2028 · this volume's date line |

The count is always from a threshold. When a document in the deposit says "the third spring," it means 2027. The copyright page says 2026. The Editor has noticed, and has printed it as found.

## Coherence notes for the workshop (not for the reader)

* **The three edges are intentional.** 24 February (confirmed), 14 March (sworn), and 26 March (the keyboard) are three standards of proof. The Editor's annotation in Record II, box 4, folder 2 states this outright. Every "forty-one days" in the book counts from 26 March to the morning of May 6.
* **The engagement and the ingest share a week.** The engagement letter is dated Friday 9 April 2027. The ingest window counts its weeks from that Friday, so the packet on 24 June falls in week 11, Alex's notice on 5 July in week 13, and the deposit on 13 July in week 14.
* **The key was committed before it was asked for.** Room i's sentence entered the orchestrator's flags file on 27 January 2027, three hours before Hale asked for a seal. The seal itself was made on 19 February, twenty-three days later.
* **The real record stops on 16 October 2026.** The Committee sittings (December 2025, 31 March, 4 August, and 3 October 2026) and every Watchtower figure in Record I, Record V, and Appendix C come from the repository as it stood on that date. The December Audit is real and has not yet sat; the novel's binders mark it *after*.
* **The date line is the fourth spring.** The volume records the second seal of 14 December 2027 as "unopened at press," so the Editor's Note and the imprint are dated 2028. The copyright page's 2026 is the one date the Editor declines to correct.
