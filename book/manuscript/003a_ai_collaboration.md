#T The Hand and the Glass

!center:italic|On the use of generative artificial intelligence

This book began in human inquiry: in engineering, history, archival practice, and the conversations between them. Its concepts, originating writings, and intellectual commitments are mine and those of my human collaborators, whose contributions retain their own attribution. They are intellectually sovereign from the tools used to develop and present them.

The Companion Dossier is the public body of work from which this novel grew. It has two doors: a website for exploring its writings, experiments, and conversations, and a GitHub repository, the online filing room holding the source documents, code, and record of changes. Begin with the website; turn to the repository to look behind it. Both addresses appear on the preceding page.

Generative AI served as a dialogic instrument in building that work. I supplied the writing, seeds, questions, and direction; Claude was the primary system through which I developed and assembled the repository. The dialogue helped me articulate, test, organize, and elaborate the material. The purposes of the inquiry and the judgment governing it remained human.

The summoned voices belong here as well. The exchanges gathered under From Beyond show the project using the method it studies: personae called into dialogue around a humanly supplied seed. These are AI-mediated voices, not testimony from the people whose names they bear. The instrument was used from within, and its traces were kept.

For the novel, I opened a Claude Code session with the entire repository, together with dozens of my handwritten notes and text files that constituted a rough outline of the book. A conservative estimate puts the repository’s readable text alone at more than three hundred printed pages. I worked through my conception in dialogue with Claude and used it to develop an initial draft from those materials and my direction. I read and revised that draft, then used later versions of Claude to workshop and strengthen the manuscript. Generated prose and editorial assistance belonged to a process I conceived, directed, and revised.

OpenAI tools assisted with editorial refinement, book design, typesetting, and production; OpenAI image tools were used for the cover artwork. The frontispiece and five record-opening plates are typeset presentations of material in this volume. The estate map was redrawn from the deposited plate.

Authorship here rests in intellectual responsibility and accountability. I attach my name, credentials, and standing to this edition and answer for its substance, including its errors and omissions. My collaborators retain credit for their contributions. The tools hold no authorial responsibility.

The novel asks what a voice can carry, and what the record can warrant. Its instruments are disclosed because the distinction matters within the work itself. The glass is shown. The hand remains answerable.
