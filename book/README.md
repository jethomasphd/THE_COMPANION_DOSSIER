## KDP settings

- Binding: hardcover, case laminate.
- Trim size: 6 × 9 inches.
- Interior: premium color on white paper.
- Page count: 304.
- Interior bleed: no bleed.
- Cover finish: matte recommended.
- Cover: **A · Archive Sculpture**, selected by the author.
- Full cover size: 14.477 × 10.417 inches; spine: 0.902 inch.
- Barcode: leave the existing-barcode option unchecked so KDP can add the ISBN barcode in the reserved white panel.

The interior includes the revised *The Hand and the Glass*, the documentary evidence plates and estate map, the corrected introduction to Hale’s October illness, and the acknowledgment naming **The Halpern Memo**.

Local print-file checks passed. KDP Print Previewer and a physical proof remain to be checked; no KDP upload has been performed.
