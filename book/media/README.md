## Files and use

- Both folders include posters, scripts/timing, and selectable caption files. Captions are also composed into the films.
- The repository uses the 720p website encodes of the 90-second trailers. The portrait promos are 1080 × 1920. Full-resolution 1080p trailer masters and editable source media are preserved in the local launch kit.
- The original website trailer, portrait promos, and original local masters are preserved without content changes. The original website assets were moved from `book/assets/` into `book/media/original/` for clarity.
- The shared destination is **the-companion-dossier.com/book**. Amazon purchasing is enabled through `book/config.js` when the listing is ready.
- These are promotions for a **novel**. The scientist, disappearance, and uncanny events belong to the fiction. The real Companion Dossier remains available to explore.
- The digital book is free. Print is the intended experience; purchasing supports continued development of the Companion Dossier's product, scholarship, and moral framework.

Moving footage, narration, and instrumental score were made with generative tools. The book's selected cover and estate typography are reused with their existing attribution. See each collection's scripts and production notes for its construction.
