USING THIS MATTER / BOOK SITE: "THE DEPOSIT"

Destination: https://the-companion-dossier.com/book

The page is a static part of the existing repository and Cloudflare Pages
site. It needs no framework, package install, server function, or new hosting
service, and the existing build.sh continues unchanged. Nothing outside book/
was modified.

THE CONCEPT
The site is part of the fiction. It presents the novel the way the novel
presents itself: as a recovered archive. Visitors meet in-world artifacts
before they meet the pitch: an estate terminal, recovered posts, an interactive
county plan, transcripts that "play", a withheld plate, and a hidden door.
Every in-world element is framed honestly. The recovered posts carry an
Editor's disclosure, the FAQ says plainly what is fiction, and no review, price,
date, or stock state is invented.

ACTIVATE AMAZON AT PUBLICATION (unchanged contract)
1. Open book/config.js.
2. Paste the final, published Amazon book-page URL between the quotes in
   amazonUrl: ""
3. Commit and publish through the site's existing deployment process.
4. Open /book on a phone and desktop. Check that the hero's "Hold it in print"
   button and the access tier's Amazon button both reach the right edition.

A valid HTTPS Amazon marketplace or amzn.to URL reveals the Amazon button,
retargets every [data-print-cta], hides the "forthcoming" notice, and updates
the availability line. Blank or unsupported keeps the honest prepublication
state: print forthcoming, digital free.

SPOILER POLICY
Copy stays inside the premise: the commission, the house, the count, the
rite, the founding session, the Prism's design, the existence of the
interruption (its sentences are redacted on the site), the withheld plate,
and the existence of the second hand. Record III–V copy uses only the Editor's
Note and the plate captions. The posts never reference the deposit's contents
beyond Record II. See campaign/README.md §3 for the rules of the fiction.

MEASUREMENT
The page dispatches local DOM CustomEvents named book:engagement, with
name, placement and incoming utm_* values. Names: free_read, amazon_click,
trailer_play, volume_turn, count_twelve, count_recount, evidence_filter,
evidence_more, thread_open, transmission_play. Nothing is transmitted and no
cookies or storage are used. Connect analytics separately if wanted.

ACCESSIBILITY AND MOTION
The 3D book is keyboard-turnable (arrows, Enter). Doors are focusable buttons
with labels, and the count log is a live region. Transcripts fall back to
static text. prefers-reduced-motion disables grain, ticker, rings, glitch,
typing and reveals. Layout checked at 390 and 1440 px with no horizontal
overflow. The videos keep preload="none" and their caption tracks.

PREVIEW
From the repository root: python -m http.server 8765 --bind 127.0.0.1
Then open http://127.0.0.1:8765/book/ (and /book/twelve/, /book/media/).

Fonts: unchanged copies of the estate's Cormorant Garamond, Crimson Pro, and
