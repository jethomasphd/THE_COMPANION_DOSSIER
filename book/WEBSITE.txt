USING THIS MATTER / BOOK SITE: "THE DEPOSIT"

Destination: https://the-companion-dossier.com/book

The page is a static part of the existing repository and Cloudflare Pages
site. It needs no framework, package install, server function, or new hosting
service, and the existing build.sh continues unchanged. Nothing outside book/
was modified.

THE CONCEPT
The site is part of the fiction. It presents the novel the way the novel
presents itself: as a recovered archive. Visitors meet in-world artifacts
before they meet the pitch: an estate terminal, recovered posts, the book's
own floor plan, transcripts that "play", a withheld plate, and a hidden door.
Every in-world element is framed honestly. The recovered posts carry an
Editor's disclosure, the FAQ says plainly what is fiction, and no review, price,
date, or stock state is invented.

ACTIVATE AMAZON AT PUBLICATION (unchanged contract)
1. Open book/config.js.
2. Paste the final, published Amazon book-page URL between the quotes in
   amazonUrl: ""
3. Commit and publish through the site's existing deployment process.
4. Open /book on a phone and desktop. Check that the hero's "Hold it in print"
   button and the access tier's Amazon button both reach the right edition.

A valid HTTPS Amazon marketplace or amzn.to URL reveals the Amazon button,
retargets every [data-print-cta], hides the "forthcoming" notice, and updates
the availability line. Blank or unsupported keeps the honest prepublication
state: print forthcoming, digital free.

PAGE STRUCTURE (book/index.html), built for cold traffic from social ads
A visitor should know within five seconds that this is a horror novel,
what it is about, and that the whole book is free to read.
  Header      — title + "Read free" (on phones: title only, plus a sticky
                bottom bar with cover, title and "Read free")
  Hero        — "A horror novel" chip, title, hook ("He built an AI to talk to
                the dead. Then he disappeared."), one-paragraph premise,
                format line ("All 304 pages are free to read online"),
                Read it free + Watch the trailer, key facts, turnable 3D book
  Comps strip — For readers of House of Leaves, S., Annihilation, Black
                Mirror, The Blair Witch Project
  Story       — the story in 30 seconds: the scientist, the house, the
                archivist (+ case-file terminal on desktop)
  First page  — the opening page verbatim, page thumbnails, "Turn the page"
  Trailer     — ONE film only: the 90-second story trailer, custom play
                button, AI-imagery note, transcript
  Four things that shouldn't be there — the extra door, the Franklin test
                (typed transcript), the 3:11:47 interruption (sentences
                blacked out), the withheld seventh illustration
  The plan    — Plate XI, the floor plan, as a still image straight from
                the book (tap to enlarge)
  Posts       — "None of these people exist." Fictional reader reviews,
                reader videos and threads, blended with recovered in-story
                posts from 2027; filters; visible fiction notice; a
                "◈ fiction" tag inside every card
  Read        — one offer: the full novel free (PDF served by this site),
                hardcover as a one-line "coming soon" that switches to an
                Amazon link via config.js
  FAQ + author, footer with the hidden "Twelve?" link

THE FREE BOOK LINK
Every "Read it free" button opens USING_THIS_MATTER_KDP_interior_304pp.pdf
from this site (/book/), not GitHub, so ad traffic never lands on a code page.

SPOILER POLICY
Copy stays inside the premise: the commission, the house, the count, the
rite, the Franklin test, the Prism's design, the existence of the
interruption (its sentences are blacked out on the site), the withheld
illustration, and the existence of the unsigned second annotator. Nothing
reveals Condition IV, the freezer, the sealed record, who hired Miranda, or the
ending. Every post and review is fiction and labeled as such; see
campaign/README.md §3.

MEASUREMENT
The page dispatches local DOM CustomEvents named book:engagement, with
name, placement and incoming utm_* values. Names: free_read, amazon_click,
trailer_play, trailer_jump, volume_turn, evidence_filter,
evidence_more, thread_open, transmission_play. Nothing is transmitted and no
cookies or storage are used. Connect analytics separately if wanted.

ACCESSIBILITY AND MOTION
The 3D book is keyboard-turnable (arrows, Enter). Doors are focusable buttons
and the floor plan has full alt text. Transcripts fall back to
static text. prefers-reduced-motion disables grain, ticker, rings, glitch,
typing and reveals. Layout checked at 390 and 1440 px with no horizontal
overflow. The videos keep preload="none" and their caption tracks.

PREVIEW
From the repository root: python -m http.server 8765 --bind 127.0.0.1
Then open http://127.0.0.1:8765/book/ (and /book/twelve/, /book/media/).

Fonts: unchanged copies of the estate's Cormorant Garamond, Crimson Pro, and

font, script, or analytics requests are made.
