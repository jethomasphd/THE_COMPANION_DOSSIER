USING THIS MATTER / BOOK SITE: "THE DEPOSIT"

Destination: https://the-companion-dossier.com/book

The page is a static part of the existing repository and Cloudflare Pages
site. It needs no framework, package install, server function, or new hosting
service, and the existing build.sh continues unchanged. Nothing outside book/
was modified.

THE CONCEPT
The site is part of the fiction. It presents the novel the way the novel
presents itself: as a recovered archive. Visitors meet in-world artifacts
before they meet the pitch: an estate terminal, recovered posts, the book's
own floor plan, transcripts that "play", a withheld plate, and a hidden door.
Every in-world element is framed honestly. The recovered posts carry an
Editor's disclosure, the FAQ says plainly what is fiction, and no review, price,
date, or stock state is invented.

THE CONVERSION: THE HARDCOVER (book/config.js)
The page is built to end at Door 12, the hardcover. One setting decides what
every primary button does.
  amazonUrl blank (now)  — primary buttons read "Read it free" and open the
                           free PDF; Door 12 says the hardcover is coming soon
                           and offers the free edition tonight.
  amazonUrl set          — every primary button (header, hero, author, phone
                           dock, Door 12) becomes "Get the hardcover" and opens
                           Amazon; the free PDF stays as a quiet text link; the
                           availability lines and ticker update.
  notifyUrl (optional)   — before publication only: Door 12 adds "Tell me when
                           it ships" (any https link: a mailing-list signup or
                           a preorder page).
To publish: paste the Amazon URL (https://www.amazon.… or https://amzn.to/…),
commit, deploy, then open /book on a phone and a desktop and follow the
hardcover buttons once.

THE POSTS
In-world only. No fictional reader reviews, ratings or testimonials, on the
site or in the campaign; social proof is the real talk, paper and repository.
The voice memo (assets/posts/memo-014.m4a; in the feed as memo-014.mp4, a
720x900 recorder-screen video built from the same audio) is machine speech placed in a
recorded-at-night scene. Speech: Piper neural TTS, running locally, voice
en_US-libritts_r-medium, speaker 298 of 904 (LibriTTS-R audiobook readers,
CC BY 4.0, openslr.org/141). Each phrase is synthesized separately with its own
pacing, made hushed (breath blended in, softer top end, a slight tremor) and
mixed with the garage hum, crickets that stop when the car passes, wind on the
mic, footsteps and a screen door, through an emulated phone mic and automatic
gain control. Intelligibility is measured, not assumed: every phrase is
re-recorded until three Whisper recognizers (base/small/medium) hear exactly the
scripted words, then the best of several full takes is chosen by scoring a
decode of the final AAC file. Rebuild: python book/campaign/audio/make_memo_014.py
--punch --takes 5, then node book/campaign/audio/apply_memo_014.js, then
--feed book/campaign/audio/feed-bg.jpg (feed video), --reel
book/campaign/audio/reel-bg.jpg (social reel) and --ambient (room tone).

SPOILER POLICY
Copy stays inside the premise: the commission, the house, the count, the
rite, the Franklin test, the Prism's design, the existence of the
interruption (its sentences are blacked out on the site), the withheld
illustration, and the existence of the unsigned second annotator. Nothing
reveals Condition IV, the freezer, the sealed record, who hired Miranda, or the
ending. See campaign/README.md §3 for the rules of the fiction.

MEASUREMENT
The page dispatches local DOM CustomEvents named book:engagement, with
name, placement and incoming utm_* values. Names: free_read, amazon_click,
trailer_play, trailer_jump, volume_turn, memo_play, session_replay, alert_shown, sound_on, notify_click, proof, evidence_more,
evidence_more, thread_open, transmission_play. Nothing is transmitted and no
cookies or storage are used. Connect analytics separately if wanted.

ACCESSIBILITY AND MOTION
The 3D book is keyboard-turnable (arrows, Enter). Doors are focusable buttons
and the floor plan has full alt text. Transcripts fall back to
static text. prefers-reduced-motion disables grain, ticker, rings, glitch,
typing and reveals. Layout checked at 390 and 1440 px with no horizontal
overflow. The videos keep preload="none" and their caption tracks.

PREVIEW
From the repository root: python -m http.server 8765 --bind 127.0.0.1
Then open http://127.0.0.1:8765/book/ (and /book/twelve/, /book/media/).
