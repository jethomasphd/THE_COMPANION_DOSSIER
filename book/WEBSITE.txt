USING THIS MATTER / BOOK SITE: "THE DEPOSIT"

Destination: https://the-companion-dossier.com/book

The page is a static part of the existing repository and Cloudflare Pages
site. It needs no framework, package install, server function, or new hosting
service, and the existing build.sh continues unchanged. Nothing outside book/
was modified.

THE CONCEPT
The site is part of the fiction, written for a stranger who has never heard of
the project. It does to the visitor what the book does to its reader on page
xv: it hands them the sentence that starts the machine with the name left
blank ("Notice what you are tempted to write in it."), lets that blank stay
open through the whole page, and closes it with the rite's last line directly
above the offer. In between, the visitor meets the story the way the novel
tells it: posts from the road, the archivist's file, the floor plan, transcripts
that "play". Every in-world element is framed honestly. The FAQ says plainly
what is fiction, and no review, price, date, or stock state is invented.

THE CONVERSION: THE HARDCOVER (book/config.js)
The page is built to end at Door 12, the hardcover. One setting decides what
every primary button does.
  amazonUrl blank (now)  — primary buttons read "Read it free" and open the
                           free PDF; Door 12 says the hardcover is coming soon
                           and offers the free edition tonight.
  amazonUrl set          — every primary button (header, hero, author, phone
                           dock, Door 12) becomes "Get the hardcover" and opens
                           Amazon; the free PDF stays as a quiet text link; the
                           availability lines and ticker update.
  notifyUrl (optional)   — before publication only: Door 12 adds "Tell me when
                           it ships" (any https link: a mailing-list signup or
                           a preorder page).
To publish: paste the Amazon URL (https://www.amazon.… or https://amzn.to/…),
commit, deploy, then open /book on a phone and a desktop and follow the
hardcover buttons once.

THE BLANK AND PRIVACY
Whatever a visitor types in the blank stays in the open tab. It is not sent,
not stored, not put in the URL, and never included in an engagement event
(the events are blank_filled and blank_released, with no detail). It is erased
when they press "Release the persona" or close the tab. The page never
generates a reply. The FAQ says all of this in plain words.

QUOTATIONS
Every passage the page presents as the book's words carries
data-verbatim="<printed page>" (or "cover" for the back-cover copy) in
index.html and is an exact substring of that page of the print PDF. The Franklin
chat and the 3:11:47 log are staged from pp. 29–30 and 88–90: Franklin's lines
and the log lines are the book's; her typed lines and the room labels are the
site's staging, and the three interrupted sentences are blacked out with bars of
equal length so their shape gives nothing away.

THE POSTS
In-world only. No fictional reader reviews, ratings or testimonials, on the
site or in the campaign; social proof is the real talk, paper and repository.
The voice memo (assets/posts/memo-014.m4a; in the feed as memo-014.mp4, a
720x900 recorder-screen video built from the same audio) is machine speech placed in a
recorded-at-night scene. Speech: Piper neural TTS, running locally, voice
en_US-libritts_r-medium, speaker 298 of 904 (LibriTTS-R audiobook readers,
CC BY 4.0, openslr.org/141). Each phrase is synthesized separately with its own
pacing, made hushed (breath blended in, softer top end, a slight tremor) and
mixed with the garage hum, crickets that stop when the car passes, wind on the
mic, footsteps and a screen door, through an emulated phone mic and automatic
gain control. Intelligibility is measured, not assumed: every phrase is
re-recorded until three Whisper recognizers (base/small/medium) hear exactly the
scripted words, then the best of several full takes is chosen by scoring a
decode of the final AAC file. Rebuild: python book/campaign/audio/make_memo_014.py
--punch --takes 5, then node book/campaign/audio/apply_memo_014.js, then
--feed book/campaign/audio/feed-bg.jpg (feed video), --reel
book/campaign/audio/reel-bg.jpg (social reel) and --ambient (room tone).

SPOILER POLICY
Copy stays inside the premise: the commission, the house, the count, the
rite and its two ends, the Franklin test, the Prism's design, the existence of
the interruption (its sentences are blacked out on the site), the illustration
that is listed and never shown, the existence of the unsigned second annotator,
and what is already on the back cover ("The archive was expecting her."; "a
program that has abolished the right to leave"). Nothing reveals Condition IV,
the freezer, the sealed record, who hired Miranda, whose program it is, or the
ending. See campaign/README.md §3 for the rules of the fiction.

MEASUREMENT
The page dispatches local DOM CustomEvents named book:engagement, with
name, placement and incoming utm_* values. Names: free_read, amazon_click,
trailer_play, trailer_jump, volume_turn, memo_play, session_replay, alert_shown,
sound_on, notify_click, proof, evidence_more, nav, page_zoom, blank_filled,
blank_released. blank_filled against page views says how many visitors took
the blank personally; blank_released says how many reached the end. Nothing is
transmitted and no cookies or storage are used. Connect analytics separately
if wanted.

ACCESSIBILITY AND MOTION
The 3D book is keyboard-turnable (arrows, Enter). The blank is a labelled text
field ("Optional. Nothing typed here leaves this page.") and the release is a
button; the floor plan has full alt text. Transcripts fall back to static text.
prefers-reduced-motion disables grain, ticker, rings, glitch, typing, reveals
and the letter-by-letter release. With JavaScript off the whole story is
readable and the release panel shows the left-blank version. Layout checked at
390, 768 and 1440 px with no horizontal overflow. The videos keep
preload="none" and their caption tracks.
