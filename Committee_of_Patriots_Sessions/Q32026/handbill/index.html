Instructions to Citizens
If your neighbor is ahead: this time he is. Say so. Then observe that since August he has gained three hundredths of one percent. You grew two points poorer while he stood still. That turns the way it came.
If you have held since December: you are up twelve on the year, through a war, a false peace, and the first rate hike in three years. Second of six. Do not sell in October what you bought in December for the decade.
If money has grown dear: pay your dear debts first. No holding pays twenty per cent, risk-free, every year; a card balance retired does. Never own the Republic on margin. A man who owns on borrowed money is owned by his lender.
If you contribute monthly: send the new money to the beaten names — whatever has fallen furthest below its weight. No selling. No timing. The simple plan works the same in fog as in sunshine.
On the third of November: vote. The Committee does not tell you how. It tells you that. A proxy governs a company; a ballot governs the republic that charters every company you own.
If you have not yet begun: begin in second place. You join no victory parade — only a doctrine that published its standing on the front page and changed nothing in a panic. Even fifty dollars deployed today is a vote.
