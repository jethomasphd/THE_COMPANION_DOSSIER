01 / THE FOUNDING IDEA

More information.
Less understanding?

The project began with a manifesto against the information flood. Its proposition was simple: a different perspective can help us ask a better question.

The COMPANION Dossier turns that idea into a practice. Give an AI a defined perspective, supply material to reason from, and make room for disagreement. The aim is to deepen your judgment.

02 / FROM IDEA TO PRACTICE

Your question.
A different conversation.

You do not need to learn the whole project to begin. Start with one question and one perspective.

  1. 01

    Bring something to think about.

    A decision, a passage, a lesson, an unfinished idea. A question is enough; documents are optional.

  2. 02

    Choose a perspective.

    Try Socrates for questions, Ada Lovelace for systems, or several personas for a conversation across viewpoints.

  3. 03

    Ask. Challenge. Follow the thread.

    Explore the responses, return to your sources, and decide what holds up. Export the conversation if you want to keep the record.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you begin.

Am I speaking to a historical person?

You are speaking to a language model generating a persona. The protocol asks it to use a particular perspective and supplied sources. Its answers can be mistaken, anachronistic, or inconsistent. The scholarship investigates how to evaluate that fidelity.

Do I need to understand AI or read the whole Dossier?

No. Start with one persona and one question in The Harness. The manifesto and research are there when you want to understand the ideas and implementation more deeply.

Is it free to explore?

The public materials and protocol are available to read and use. The hosted tools currently use a shared AI connection with usage limits. Using the protocol in another provider’s service follows that provider’s pricing and limits. The novel’s digital edition is free.

What happens to documents I add?

In The Harness, your supplied text and conversation are sent to Anthropic through The COMPANION Dossier service. Recent sessions are stored in your browser so you can resume. Exported transcripts include the documents you supplied, so review an export before sharing it.

How can I inspect, adapt, or contribute?

The protocol and implementation are on GitHub. Start with the README for setup, source links, licensing, and citations. You can open an issue with a reproducible problem, source correction, or proposal.
